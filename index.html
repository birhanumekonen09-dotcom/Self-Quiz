Question 1: Nested Absolute Values

Find all real values of x that satisfy the nested modulus equation: ||x - 2| - 3| = 5

  • A) x = {-6, 2, 10}
  • B) x = {-4, 10}
  • C) x = {-6, 10}
  • D) x = {-2, 6}

Solution & Rationale

Correct Answer: C) x = {-6, 10}
The outer modulus gives two cases: |x - 2| - 3 = 5 → x = 10 or x = -6.

Question 2: Parameter Constraints & Solutions

Determine the total number of real solutions to the equation: |x² - 4x + 3| = x - 1

  • A) 1 solution
  • B) 2 solutions
  • C) 3 solutions
  • D) 4 solutions

Solution & Rationale

Correct Answer: C) 3 solutions
Factoring yields |(x - 1)(x - 3)| = x - 1, producing valid solutions at x = 1, 2, and 4.

Question 3: Sum of Solutions

Find the sum of all real solutions to the equation: |2x - 3| + |x + 1| = 6

  • A) 4/3
  • B) 2
  • C) 8/3
  • D) 3

Solution & Rationale

Correct Answer: A) 4/3
Testing critical intervals gives valid solutions at x = 8/3 and x = -4/3. Their sum is 4/3.

Question 4: Geometric Interpretation

For what specific real value of k does the equation |x - 1| + |x - 5| = k possess infinitely many solutions?

  • A) k = 2
  • B) k = 4
  • C) k = 6
  • D) No such value exists

Solution & Rationale

Correct Answer: B) k = 4
The distance between 1 and 5 is 4, meaning every point in the closed interval [1, 5] satisfies the equation.

Question 5: Product of Solutions

Find the product of all real solutions to the equation: |x + 2| + |x - 2| = 6

  • A) -9
  • B) -6
  • C) 9
  • D) 36

Solution & Rationale

Correct Answer: A) -9
Solving the interval cases yields x = 3 and x = -3. Their product is -9.
